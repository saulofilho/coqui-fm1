# COQUÍ changelog

## 1.1

A welcome out of the box, so a new FM-1 feels like COQUÍ from the first press.

- **The welcome scene**: a fresh FM-1 boots into a Dembow/Perreo *orquesta* — a perreo perc groove, an 808 bass, a
  night pad and a perreo pluck across the four tracks — instead of GR!MM's blank VA saw. A **DEMO** action on the
  PROJECTS page (beside LOAD / SAVE / ERASE / NEW) loads it on demand on any device.
- **A COQUÍ default sound**: on a device, a blank/NEW track plays TROPICAL SYNTH, not a raw saw, so pressing the keys
  sounds like the firmware at once.
- Device only: the host tests and the web emulator keep the clean default, so the whole test suite is unchanged.

## 1.0

The first release: COQUÍ, a Latin and Caribbean groovebox firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, built on GR!MM 2.0.2.

- **PERC**: a new source of 22 Latin and Caribbean percussion instruments, synthesized on the FM-1 (no samples), on
  the 27 keys at any octave; kits SALSA, PERREO, BOMBA, TIPICO; TUNE, DECAY, HUMAN; its own pool of ten hits, never
  taking the six voices the other tracks share.
- **GROOVE**: 14 styles a PERC track plays by itself on the sequencer's bars (dembow, perreo, salsa 2-3 and 3-2, son,
  mambo, cha-cha-chá, bomba sicá and yubá, plena, merengue, bachata, cumbia, soca), with PARTS, FILL and FEEL.
- **The lines**: the ARP plays TUMBAO, MONTUNO, GUAJEO, MERENGUE and BACHATA over the chord held (one key is enough).
- **44 factory sounds** made for it, the bass and piano ones bringing their line; FM patches BRASS, PIANO, CUATRO, BASS.
- **The mood**: GENERATE in the clave (NOCHE, CALLE, FIESTA); AMBIENTE scenes for the effects (SECO, QUINTAL, CALLE,
  PLAYA, NOCHE, DUB, RADIO AM); a night HOME screen where the coquí sings over a conga a track; a noche tropical palette
  and a singing boot screen.
- Its own identity (`FM-1_923`) and saves (it never reads another firmware's); newer FM-1s' flash (UC25HQ80) supported.
- The hub page and a web emulator: <https://saulofilho.github.io/coqui-fm1/>.
- Not from GR!MM any more: the VOICE source and its choirs, GR!MM's factory sounds, the eye, webs and candles.
